# Culture, Colour & Celebration

## A Nigerian Traditional-Wedding Photo Story

![Project cover](images/cover/wedding-cover.jpg)

## About the Project

This photography project documents a Nigerian traditional wedding through
family, culture, clothing, ceremony, portraiture and celebration.

My goal is to preserve both the major ceremonial moments and the smaller
interactions that reveal how the day felt.

## The Story

### 1. Details and Preparation

The opening photographs introduce the clothing, accessories, environment
and preparations that shaped the visual identity of the celebration.

![Preparation](images/preparation/preparation-01.jpg)

### 2. Family and Community

These photographs focus on the relationships surrounding the couple,
including parents, elders, relatives, friends and members of the community.

![Family](images/family/family-01.jpg)

### 3. The Ceremony

This section documents the important ceremonial moments in the order in
which they occurred.

![Ceremony](images/ceremony/ceremony-01.jpg)

### 4. Couple Portraits

The portrait session celebrates the couple and the craftsmanship, colour
and meaning of their wedding attire.

![Couple portrait](images/portraits/portrait-01.jpg)

### 5. Celebration

The final section captures the movement, music, dancing, laughter and
energy of the celebration.

![Celebration](images/celebration/celebration-01.jpg)

## Photography Approach

I approached the wedding as a complete visual story rather than a collection
of unrelated photographs. I looked for establishing scenes, significant
details, emotional reactions, formal portraits and energetic moments.
